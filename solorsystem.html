SOLAR  SYSTEM



List of Planets

  1. Mercury
  2. Venus
  3. Earth
  4. Mars
  5. Jupiter
  6. Saturn
  7. Uranus
  8. Neptune

    Sun

  • The Sun is the star at the center of our Solar System.
  • It is made mostly of hydrogen and helium.
  • The Sun provides Earth with light and heat.
  • It is much larger than all the planets.
  • Without the Sun, life on Earth would not exist.

    Mercury

  • Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.
  • It is the smallest planet in our Solar System.
  • Its surface is rocky and covered with many craters.
  • Mercury has almost no atmosphere.
  • It has very hot days and very cold nights.

    Venus

  • Venus is the second planet from the Sun.
  • It is the hottest planet in our Solar System.
  • Its atmosphere is very thick and mostly carbon dioxide.
  • Venus is covered with clouds of sulfuric acid.
  • It is sometimes called Earth's sister planet.

    Earth

  • Earth is the third planet from the Sun.
  • It is the only known planet with life.
  • Most of Earth's surface is covered by water.
  • It has one natural satellite called the Moon.
  • Earth has an atmosphere that supports living things.

    Mars

  • Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun.
  • It is known as the Red Planet.
  • Its reddish color comes from iron-rich dust.
  • Mars has mountains, valleys, craters, and polar ice caps.
  • It has two small moons called Phobos and Deimos.

    Jupiter

  • Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System.
  • It is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium.
  • Jupiter has colorful bands of clouds.
  • The Great Red Spot is a huge storm on Jupiter.
  • It also has many moons.

    Saturn

  • Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun.
  • It is the second-largest planet in the Solar System.
  • Saturn is famous for its beautiful rings.
  • It is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium.
  • Saturn has many moons, including Titan.

    Uranus

  • Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun.
  • It is an ice giant with a blue-green color.
  • Its color comes from methane in its atmosphere.
  • Uranus rotates on its side because of its extreme tilt.
  • It also has faint rings and many moons.

    Neptune

  • Neptune is the eighth planet from the Sun.
  • It is a cold, blue ice giant.
  • Neptune has some of the fastest winds in the Solar System.
  • Methane in its atmosphere gives it a blue color.
  • It has several moons, including Triton.
